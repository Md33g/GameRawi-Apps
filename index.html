Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built as a single, self-contained app — no accounts, no permissions you didn't ask for, no third-party SDKs.

✍️

Just type

A plain-text editor with autosave and word counts. No formatting toolbar to fight — your line breaks render exactly as typed.

♻️

Safe delete

Notes go to Trash first. Restore individually, restore all at once, or empty the trash permanently when you're sure.

😀

Emoji icons

Give every note a face. Type or paste any emoji from your keyboard — even family, flags, and skin-tone combinations.

🎨

Color labels

Eight color presets tint the edge of each note card so you can spot categories at a glance.

🌍

Arabic & RTL

Type Arabic and it flows right-to-left automatically. Force LTR, RTL, or auto per note — or flip the entire app's direction.

🎬

Living backgrounds

Starfield with shooting stars, floating particles, nebula drift, or gentle waves. Adjust intensity and speed — or turn it off.

🔤

16 open-source fonts

Inter, IBM Plex, Lato, Ubuntu, Noto Naskh Arabic, Amiri, JetBrains Mono and more. All local. Zero Google Fonts.

🔐

Private by design

Everything is stored on your device. The app makes no network requests after install. No analytics, no ads, no accounts.

📦

Own your data

Export everything to a JSON backup and re-import on any device. Export individual notes as plain text.

📁

Folders & search

Organize notes into folders, pin the important ones, and search across titles and content instantly.

📝

Templates

Start from Blank, Meeting, Todo, Journal, Idea, or Recipe. Change the default in Settings.

♿

Accessible

Full keyboard navigation, ARIA labels, focus rings, and proper support for reduced-motion preferences.

Install

Up and running in under a minute.

The APK is hosted directly on GitHub Releases — no Play Store, no account required.

1

Download the APK

Tap Download APK above. The file is GameRawi.Notes.v1.0.1.apk.

2

Allow installation from your browser

Android will warn you that the app is from an unknown source. Open Settings → Apps → Your browser → Install unknown apps and toggle it on for this download only.

3

Open the APK and install

Tap the downloaded file in your notification shade or Files → Downloads, then choose Install.

4

Start writing

Launch GameRawi Notes and tap the + button. Notes save to your device automatically.

Android only. This is a sideloaded APK, not a Google Play listing. Play Protect may show an informational prompt — that's expected for any app distributed outside the store. You can always verify the file by comparing it to the asset attached to the GitHub release.

What's in v1.0.1

A focused first release — the essentials, done right.

v1.0.1 Latest
  • Plain-text editor with autosave, live word / character / reading-time counts.
  • Trash with per-note restore, Restore All, and Empty Trash.
  • Per-note emoji icon and color label.
  • Full Arabic / RTL support — auto-detect, per-note direction, whole-app RTL.
  • 12 color themes (6 free + 6 demo) and 16 FOSS font stacks.
  • Four animated backgrounds — Starfield, Particles, Nebula, Waves — with intensity and speed controls.
  • Folders, full-text search, sort order, grid or list layout.
  • JSON export/import and per-note plain-text export.
  • Six plain-text note templates (Blank, Meeting, Todo, Journal, Idea, Recipe).
  • Complete legal notice, permissions panel, and About page.
